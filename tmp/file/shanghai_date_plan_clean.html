Romantic Jing'an Date Plan: 5 Enchanting Spots Within 5km

A thoughtfully crafted 6-hour evening itinerary for couples in Shanghai's heart — blending history, art, nature, and intimacy.

1. Wukang Road — The Timeless Stroll (4:00–5:30 PM)

Begin your date on Shanghai’s most iconic romantic lane. Wukang Road — just 1 km long and lined with century-old European-style villas — invites slow walking, quiet conversation, and spontaneous photos. Sip artisanal coffee at % Arabica beneath ginkgo trees, admire the grand Wukang Mansion, and let the soft afternoon light paint golden hues across historic facades.

[Image: Wukang Road at sunset — historic villas & ginkgo trees]

2. Jing’an Sculpture Park — Art & Serenity (5:30–6:30 PM)

A short 15-minute walk or metro hop brings you to Jing’an Sculpture Park — Shanghai’s only downtown sculpture park, open 24/7 and free. Wander past monumental contemporary works, pause by the tranquil lake, and find your favorite bench. With over 100 sculptures set among bamboo groves and lotus ponds, it’s a haven for meaningful moments — and unforgettable photos.

[Image: Jing’an Sculpture Park — lakeside sculptures & bamboo groves]

3. Jing’an Temple — Peaceful Reflection (6:30–7:30 PM)

As golden hour deepens, walk 5 minutes to Jing’an Temple — a 1,700-year-old Buddhist sanctuary hidden in the city’s bustle. Enter through its vermilion gates into quiet gardens fragrant with incense. Sit together on stone steps, watch monks pass by, and absorb the gentle chime of temple bells. At dusk, the temple glows warmly — a perfect moment of stillness before dinner.

[Image: Jing’an Temple at dusk — red gates, incense smoke, garden lanterns]

4. Ferguson Lane — Elegant Dinner (7:30–9:00 PM)

Step into Ferguson Lane, where cobblestone alleys meet curated design. Reserve ahead at Fu He Hui — Shanghai’s first Michelin-starred vegetarian restaurant — for an exquisite, artistic tasting menu served in hushed elegance. Lanterns flicker overhead, service is intuitive, and every dish tells a story of harmony and seasonality.

[Image: Ferguson Lane at night — cobblestones, boutique façades, warm lantern light]

5. Daning Park — Twilight Magic (9:00–10:30 PM)

End your evening at Daning Park — Shanghai’s largest downtown green space. Stroll hand-in-hand along the illuminated "Digital Runway", cross wooden bridges over lotus lakes, and pause at a waterside café for jasmine tea. As city lights shimmer on the water and digital art pulses softly around you, toast to love — in the heart of Jing’an.

[Image: Daning Park at night — Digital Runway lights, lakeside pavilion, city skyline]
