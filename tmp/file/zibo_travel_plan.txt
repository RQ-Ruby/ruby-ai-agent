Zibo Travel Plan: 3-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Immersive History in Zhoucun Ancient Town
- Morning: Arrive in Zibo and check into Zhiweizhai Hotel (located near Zhoucun Ancient Commercial Town for convenience).
- Afternoon: Explore Zhoucun Ancient Town — nicknamed "World's First Village" and "Golden Zhoucun." Wander Ming Dynasty streets, visit historic merchant houses, and watch traditional dyeing demonstrations.
- Evening: Dinner at a local barbecue restaurant — try Zibo's famous interactive barbecue (pre-cooked skewers grilled at your table with small pancakes and dipping sauce).

Day 2: Nature & Modern Parks
- Morning: Visit Wenchang Lake Tourist Resort — enjoy lakeside strolls, photo ops, and relaxing views (best during sunny days when the lake sparkles).
- Afternoon: Head to Torch Park, Zibo's top modern attraction. Explore its gardens, cultural exhibits, and scenic walking routes.
- Evening: Sample local snacks like tanghulu (candied hawthorn), douzhi (fermented soybean drink), and malt sugar at Zhoucun's night market.

Day 3: Cultural Deep Dive & Departure
- Morning: Revisit Zhoucun for souvenir shopping (silk products, sugar figurines) and a quiet coffee at a lakeside café near Wenchang Lake.
- Late Morning: Optional visit to Baitian Lake or Tianxiang Park if time permits.
- Afternoon: Depart Zibo via airport or railway station (both easily accessible from Zhiweizhai Hotel).

Practical Tips:
- Best Time to Visit: Spring (March–May) for mild weather, blooming scenery, and Spring Festival events in Zhoucun.
- Transportation: Use buses (e.g., Zhangdian-Yulong line to Zhoucun) or taxis; Zhiweizhai Hotel offers easy airport/railway access.
- Must-Try Foods: Zibo barbecue (interactive grilling), silk-themed treats, and traditional snacks.
- Pro Tip: Book barbecue tables 1–2 days in advance due to high demand!
