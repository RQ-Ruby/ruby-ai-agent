Romantic Date Plan: Jing'an District, Shanghai (Romantic Plan)

Designed for couples — intimate, walkable, scenic, and deeply rooted in Shanghai's charm.

---

Location Overview
A thoughtfully paced, 5-stop romantic itinerary — all within <=5 km of Jing'an Temple, easily accessible on foot or via metro (Line 2/12/13). Perfect for a relaxed Sunday, special anniversary, or first meaningful date.

Total time: ~6-8 hours (flexible start/end)
Walking distance: ~3.5 km (leisurely pace — benches & cafes en route)
Metro needed? Only for The Bund (2 stops from Jing'an Temple) — optional if you prefer strolling along Nanjing West Road.

---

Recommended Route & Timing

1. Morning Serenity: Jing'an Temple & Sculpture Park (10:00 - 11:30 AM)
- Why: Begin with quiet reflection and timeless beauty. The temple's 1,200-year-old history, ginkgo-lined courtyard, and adjacent modern sculpture park create a poetic contrast.
- Romantic touch: Light an incense stick together; write wishes on red paper slips at the Wishing Pavilion.
- Photo tip: Capture your silhouette against the golden temple gate at sunrise light — or use the festive carousel square (as seen in our official image) for joyful, colorful shots.
- Nearby cafe: % Arabica (3-min walk, Nanjing West Rd) — try their Kyoto-style matcha latte.

2. Whimsical Charm: Moller Villa (12:00 - 1:30 PM)
- Why: A real-life fairy-tale mansion built in 1936 — half-timbered gables, stained glass, and storybook gardens.
- Romantic touch: Book lunch at The Villa Cafe (reservations recommended); request a table by the rose garden window.
- Photo tip: Pose under the Gothic archway or on the spiral staircase — soft natural light inside makes every photo glow.
- Fun fact: Built after the owner's daughter dreamed of a castle — a beautiful metaphor for love's imagination.

3. Art & Stroll: Hengshan Road Bar Street (3:00 - 4:30 PM)
- Why: Tree-shaded European architecture, boutique galleries, and cozy wine bars — perfect for unhurried conversation.
- Romantic touch: Stop at L'Amour Chocolat for handcrafted dark-chocolate truffles (Shanghai-made, rose-hinted).
- Photo tip: Golden hour light filters through plane trees — ideal for candid, film-like portraits.
- Bonus: Pop into Museum of Neon Signs (free entry) — nostalgic, playful, and full of retro Shanghai charm.

4. Iconic Vista: Garden Bridge & Suzhou Creek (5:30 - 6:30 PM)
- Why: Waibaidu Bridge is Shanghai's oldest steel bridge — elegant, historic, and framed by water, willows, and the Bund skyline.
- Romantic touch: Share a warm shengjian bao (pan-fried bun) from a riverside stall while watching boats glide by.
- Photo tip: Sunset reflections on the creek + bridge silhouette = instant postcard. Bring a portable mini-tripod!

5. Grand Finale: The Bund at Dusk (7:30 - 9:00 PM)
- Why: The undisputed crown jewel — dazzling Pudong skyscrapers across the Huangpu River, historic colonial buildings behind you, gentle river breeze.
- Romantic touch: Walk eastward from Garden Bridge toward The Bund's central promenade. Time your arrival for blue hour (just after sunset) — when lights gently awaken.
- Photo tip: Use long-exposure mode (or Night Mode on iPhone) to capture light trails of passing cars + shimmering tower lights.
- End on magic: Grab two takeaway osmanthus jelly cups from Xiao Yang Sheng Jian kiosk — sweet, fragrant, and utterly Shanghai.

---

Pro Tips for Your Date
- Best season: March-May or September-November — mild, clear, blooming gardens.
- Rainy day backup: Swap outdoor stops for Shanghai Propaganda Poster Art Centre (10-min walk from Jing'an Temple) or K11 Art Mall (art-filled, cafe-rich, indoor oasis).
- Photo gear: Bring a power bank! Most spots offer free Wi-Fi (look for 'Shanghai Free WiFi').
- Extra romance: Print this plan as a keepsake — or tuck a handwritten note inside at the first stop.
